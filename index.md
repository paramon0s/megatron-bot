# Megatron — Personal Assistant Bot

Megatron is a **private** Discord assistant bot operated solely by Marc Brookes
(marcbrookes@me.com). It is not a commercial service and is not distributed to the public.

## Terms of Service
- The bot may be invited only to servers owned or administered by the operator.
- The bot responds to messages directed at it (mentions) and performs tasks on request.
- Use of the bot is at your own discretion; no guarantee of availability or accuracy is provided.
- The operator may update or discontinue the bot at any time.

## Privacy Policy
- Messages the bot processes are used only to fulfil the request in the conversation context.
- Conversation/session data is stored **locally on the operator's own server** and is not sold or shared.
- The bot does not harvest, index, or retain server member data beyond what is needed to operate.
- Message content is forwarded to third-party AI model providers solely to generate responses.
- For any question or data removal request, contact: **marcbrookes@me.com**
